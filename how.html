How

You have to be a year 12 or 13 to join, mainly just ask around at school, and talk to Mr Sam.


The gaming club plays Valorant, an fps game. To play, just hop on a computer and pick an agent to learn idealy. In Valorant there are 4 roles, and teams of five. A well balenced team has at least one duelest, controller, initiator, and sentinal. Since Valo is a team game, its best to connect with your teamates and coordinate util to gain an easy advantage. The most common game mode is ranked, where "rr" determines your rank. The ranks are, from lowest to highest - iron - bronze - silver - gold - platnum - diamond - asendant - immortal - radiant
